title: "Public Websites"
description: "IrisAgent can scrape your public websites for AI training"
---

IrisAgent provides a specialized web scraper designed for extracting data from public websites. It allows you to ingest documentation, tutorials, blogs, and other web content into IrisAgent.

## Upload Custom Content

1. Go to **Data Sources** → **Website** on the IrisAgent dashboard.
2. Open the **Website Content** card.
3. Add the top-level URL to your website. This page and its subpages will be crawled and indexed.
