---
title: "Public Websites"
description: "Crawl your public docs, tutorials and blogs so IrisAgent can answer from content already on your site"
---

IrisAgent can crawl your public website (documentation, tutorials, release notes, blog posts) and use it in answers, without copying it into a knowledge base first.

For content behind a login or in an internal wiki, use [Confluence](/data-sources/Confluence) or [Upload Content](/data-sources/Upload-Content) instead.

## Add a website

1. Go to **Data Sources → Website** on the [IrisAgent dashboard](https://web.irisagent.com/).
2. Open the **Website Content** card.
3. Add the top-level URL. That page and its subpages are crawled and indexed.

<Tip>
Use the most specific root that covers what you need, such as your docs root rather than your marketing homepage. This keeps the index focused on pages that answer support questions.
</Tip>

## Keep content current

The crawler revisits your site to pick up new and updated pages. To pick up a change sooner, re-run the crawl from the **Website Content** card.

## Related data sources

- [Ticketing System KB](/data-sources/Ticketing-Provider-KB): your ticketing system's knowledge base
- [Confluence](/data-sources/Confluence): internal Atlassian knowledge
- [Upload Content](/data-sources/Upload-Content): CSV and PDF files

## Data governance and security

Data between your environment and IrisAgent is encrypted at rest and in transit, authenticated at each endpoint, monitored for integrity and protected by enterprise-grade security protocols.

- IrisAgent only crawls pages publicly reachable from the URL you provide.
- IrisAgent removes PII and does not store PII on its servers.
- IrisAgent does not share data with third parties.
- IrisAgent is SOC 2 Type II compliant and GDPR compliant.
