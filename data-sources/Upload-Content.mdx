Upload content that doesn't live in a connected system, such as product one-pagers, runbooks, policy documents, or FAQ spreadsheets. IrisAgent indexes it with your other sources and uses it in answers and article suggestions for agents and customers.

If the content is already in a connected system, use that integration instead. It stays in sync automatically.

## Upload files

1. Go to **Data Sources → Files** on the [IrisAgent dashboard](https://web.irisagent.com/).
2. Open **CSV Articles** (UTF-8 encoded) or **PDF Documents**, select your file, and upload it.

The content is available to IrisAgent once indexing finishes.

To add short questions and answers without a file, use the **Text Snippets** card on the same page.

## Keep content current

Uploaded files don't re-sync. Re-upload a file when the document changes. For content that changes often, use a live integration.

## Data governance and security

Data between customer environments and IrisAgent's systems is always encrypted at rest and in transit, authenticated at each endpoint, monitored for integrity, and protected by enterprise-grade security protocols.

- IrisAgent removes PII data and does not store any PII data on our servers.
- IrisAgent does not share any data with third parties.
- IrisAgent is SOC 2 Type II compliant and GDPR compliant.
