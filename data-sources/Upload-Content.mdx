## Overview

Not every piece of useful support knowledge lives in a ticketing system or a
knowledge base. Product one-pagers, internal runbooks, policy documents, and
exported FAQ spreadsheets are often scattered across drives and wikis.

File upload lets you bring that content into IrisAgent directly. Once uploaded,
it is indexed alongside your other data sources and used to suggest articles and
resolutions that match an incoming support case, for both support agents and
customers.

Use this option when the content is not already reachable through one of the
connected integrations. If the content does live in a connected system, prefer
that integration instead, because it stays in sync automatically as the source
is updated.

## Upload Custom Content

1. Go to **Data Sources** → **Files** on the [IrisAgent dashboard](https://web.irisagent.com/).
2. Open **CSV Articles** or **PDF Documents**, select your file, and click the corresponding upload button.

Uploaded files are processed and indexed after upload. Once indexing completes,
the content becomes available to IrisAgent's answers and article suggestions.

## Keeping uploaded content current

Uploaded files are a point-in-time snapshot. They do not re-sync on their own, so
re-upload the file whenever the underlying document changes materially. For
content that changes often, a live integration is a better fit than a file upload.

## Data governance and security

Data between customer environments and IrisAgent's systems is always encrypted at
rest and during transmission, authenticated at each endpoint, monitored for
integrity, and protected by enterprise-grade security protocols.

- IrisAgent removes PII data and does not store any PII data on our servers.
- IrisAgent does not share any data with third parties.
- IrisAgent is SOC 2 Type II compliant and GDPR compliant.
