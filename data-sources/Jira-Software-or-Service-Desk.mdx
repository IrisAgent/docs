## Connect Jira to IrisAgent

1. Go to the [IrisAgent Dashboard](https://web.irisagent.com/) and sign in with Google, Microsoft, or Okta SSO.

   <img
     style={{ borderRadius:"0.5rem" }}
     src="/images/web-login.png"
     alt="IrisAgent web login"
   />

2. Choose Jira as your ticket source:
   - **During onboarding:** on **Connect your first data source**, choose **Ticketing system**, then **Jira Service Management** (this also works for Jira Software).
   - **Later, or alongside another source:** go to **Data Sources → Ticketing System** and pick **Jira Service Management**.
3. Create an Atlassian API token with `write:jira-work` permission ([Atlassian's guide](https://support.atlassian.com/atlassian-account/docs/manage-api-tokens-for-your-atlassian-account/)).
4. Enter the token, the email that created it, and your subdomain (the `yourcompany` in `yourcompany.atlassian.net`). If you use a scoped API token, also enter your **Cloud ID**. Click **Connect**.

To add a second Jira account for engineering issues while keeping this one as your ticketing source, see [Connect a second Jira account](/data-sources/Jira-Software#connect-a-second-jira-account). For other platforms, go to **Data Sources** and open the relevant section.

## Install the IrisAgent app into your Jira account

1. Open [IrisAgent's listing](https://marketplace.atlassian.com/apps/1229507/irisagent?tab=overview&hosting=cloud) on the Atlassian Marketplace.
2. Click **Install** and follow the prompts.
