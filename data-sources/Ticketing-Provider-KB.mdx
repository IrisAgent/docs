## Overview

Most ticketing systems ship with their own knowledge base: Zendesk Guide,
Salesforce Knowledge, Intercom Articles, Freshworks Solutions, and the equivalent
in other platforms. This is usually the single most valuable knowledge source you
have, because it is the content your team already maintains and points customers
at every day.

IrisAgent treats it as a first-class data source.

## No additional setup required

Once you connect IrisAgent with your primary ticketing system, it automatically
connects with that system's knowledge base. There is no separate integration to
authorize and no second set of credentials to manage. The OAuth permissions you
grant during the initial ticketing connection cover both tickets and knowledge
content.

If you have not connected a ticketing system yet, start with the guide for your
platform:

- [Zendesk](/data-sources/Zendesk)
- [Salesforce](/data-sources/Salesforce)
- [Intercom](/data-sources/Intercom)
- [Freshworks](/data-sources/Freshworks)
- [HubSpot](/data-sources/Hubspot)
- [Jira Software or Service Desk](/data-sources/Jira-Software-or-Service-Desk)
- [Zoho Desk](/data-sources/Zoho)

## How the content is used

Articles from your ticketing system's knowledge base are indexed and used to
suggest resolutions that match an incoming support case, to ground chatbot
answers, and to help both support agents and customers reach a resolution faster.

Because the connection is live rather than a one-time import, edits and new
articles published in your ticketing system flow through to IrisAgent without a
manual re-upload.

## Adding knowledge from elsewhere

Your ticketing system's knowledge base is rarely the whole picture. You can layer
in additional sources:

- [Public Websites](/data-sources/Public-Websites) for docs, tutorials, and blogs
- [Confluence](/data-sources/Confluence) for internal knowledge in Atlassian
- [Upload Content](/data-sources/Upload-Content) for CSV and PDF files

## Data governance and security

Data between customer environments and IrisAgent's systems is always encrypted at
rest and during transmission, authenticated at each endpoint, and monitored for
integrity.

- IrisAgent removes PII data and does not store any PII data on our servers.
- IrisAgent does not share any data with third parties.
- IrisAgent is SOC 2 Type II compliant and GDPR compliant.
