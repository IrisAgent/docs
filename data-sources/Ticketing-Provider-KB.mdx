When you connect your ticketing system, IrisAgent also connects its knowledge base (Zendesk Guide, Salesforce Knowledge, Intercom Articles, Freshworks Solutions, and equivalents). There is no separate integration or second set of credentials. The OAuth permissions from your ticketing connection cover both tickets and articles.

If you have not connected a ticketing system yet, start here:

- [Zendesk](/data-sources/Zendesk)
- [Salesforce](/data-sources/Salesforce)
- [Intercom](/data-sources/Intercom)
- [Freshworks](/data-sources/Freshworks)
- [HubSpot](/data-sources/Hubspot)
- [Jira Software or Service Desk](/data-sources/Jira-Software-or-Service-Desk)
- [Zoho Desk](/data-sources/Zoho)

## How the content is used

IrisAgent indexes these articles to suggest resolutions for incoming cases and to ground chatbot answers. The connection is live, so new and edited articles flow into IrisAgent without a re-upload.

## Add other sources

- [Confluence](/data-sources/Confluence) for internal knowledge
- [Upload Content](/data-sources/Upload-Content) for CSV and PDF files

## Data governance and security

- Data is encrypted at rest and in transit, authenticated at each endpoint, and monitored for integrity.
- IrisAgent removes PII data and does not store any PII data on our servers.
- IrisAgent does not share any data with third parties.
- IrisAgent is SOC 2 Type II compliant and GDPR compliant.
