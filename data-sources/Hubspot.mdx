## Connect HubSpot to IrisAgent

1. Sign in to the [IrisAgent Dashboard](https://web.irisagent.com/).
   <img
     style={{ borderRadius: "0.5rem" }}
     src="/images/web-login.png"
     alt="IrisAgent web app login"
   />
2. Choose HubSpot as your ticket source:
   - **During onboarding**: on **Connect your first data source**, choose **Ticketing system**, then **HubSpot**.
   - **Later**: go to **Data Sources** > **Ticketing System** and pick **HubSpot**.
3. Enter:
   - **Hub ID** (Portal ID): in HubSpot, open the menu under your account name (top right), or copy it from your HubSpot URL.
   - **Private App Access Token** with scopes **tickets**, **sales-email-read** (ticket comments), and **crm.objects.owners.read** (agents). See [Creating Private Apps](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api/private-apps).
4. Click **Connect**.

To add other platforms (Jira, Confluence, Salesforce), open the matching section under **Data Sources**.
