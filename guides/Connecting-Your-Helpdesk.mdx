Connect your helpdesk so IrisAgent can import tickets and knowledge, learn from them, and work inside the tools your team already uses. Each step links to the detailed guide.

<Tip>
No helpdesk yet? IrisAgent includes one. See [IrisAgent Helpdesk](/helpdesk/Overview).
</Tip>

For a longer checklist (invites, procedures, AutoQA), see [Onboarding](/guides/onboarding).

## Step 1. Connect the ticketing system

1. Sign in at [web.irisagent.com](https://web.irisagent.com).
2. Complete the onboarding wizard, or go to **Data Sources → Ticketing System** and pick your provider.
3. Authorize IrisAgent (OAuth or API token, depending on the provider).
4. Wait for tickets and your helpdesk knowledge base to finish syncing.

## Step 3. Test before customers see answers

1. Open **AI Products → Chatbot** and ask real support questions.
2. Check that answers cite the articles you expect.
3. Spot-check grounding on a sample case with [Support Analyst](/support-analyst) Answer Grounding QA.
4. To tune when the AI declines to answer, read [Understanding grounded answers](/configuring-ai-and-automation/Understanding-Grounded-Answers) and [About the Hallucination Removal Engine](/configuring-ai-and-automation/Hallucination-Removal-Engine).

Low-risk first step: post AI answers as private internal notes with **Automate → Case Triggers** (see [Onboarding](/guides/onboarding)), so agents see suggestions before customers do.

## FAQ

**Do I need a separate knowledge base?**
No. Many teams start with the helpdesk KB that syncs with the ticketing connection.

**Can I connect more than one ticketing system?**
Yes. Add more from **Data Sources → Ticketing System**.

**How do I change the integration user or token?**
See [Change Integration User](/account-management/Change-Integration-User).

**Answers look wrong after connecting?**
Confirm the sync finished, check citations in the chatbot, and fix knowledge gaps before you widen automation.
