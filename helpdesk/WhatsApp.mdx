Connect a WhatsApp Business number directly to IrisAgent, and your AI agent answers every customer who messages that number. Answers are grounded in your knowledge base, in the customer's own language.

<Note>
This page covers connecting WhatsApp **directly** through the Meta WhatsApp Cloud API. If WhatsApp already runs through Zendesk or Salesforce, see [WhatsApp through Zendesk or Salesforce](/deploying-irisagent/Chatbot-Automation-on-WhatsApp) instead.
</Note>

## Before you begin

You need:

- IrisAgent admin access
- A WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) in Meta Business Manager, with a registered phone number
- A Meta **system user access token** for that WhatsApp Business Account, with the `whatsapp_business_messaging` and `whatsapp_business_management` permissions

From **WhatsApp Manager** (or your Meta app's **WhatsApp → API Setup** page), note:

- The **Phone number ID** of the number customers will message. This is a numeric ID, not the phone number itself.
- The **WhatsApp Business Account ID**.

<Tip>
Create the token for a system user rather than a personal Meta account, and give it no expiry. A token tied to a person stops working if they leave or lose access. See Meta's guide to [system users](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/503306463479099).
</Tip>

## Connect your number

1. In IrisAgent, go to **Deploy → Text channels**.
2. Find the **WhatsApp** card, below the US SMS number card.
3. Enter the **Phone number ID**.
4. Enter the **WhatsApp Business Account ID**.
5. Paste the **System user access token**.
6. Click **Connect WhatsApp**.

IrisAgent checks that the token can access the number before saving it, then stores the token encrypted. The card then shows your WhatsApp number with a **Connected** status, and AI replies are on.

You don't need to set up a webhook or callback URL. IrisAgent receives your messages automatically once the number is connected.

## Test it

1. From a personal WhatsApp account, message your business number with a common question, such as "What is your refund policy?"
2. Confirm your AI agent replies with an answer from your knowledge base.
3. In IrisAgent, open **Activity → Conversations** to see the conversation.

## Pause or resume AI replies

Use the **AI replies** toggle on the WhatsApp card:

- **Connected:** incoming WhatsApp messages are answered by your AI agent.
- **Paused:** incoming messages are received but not answered.

You don't need to re-enter the token to pause or resume.

## Change the connected number

1. On the WhatsApp card, click **Change number**.
2. Enter the new **Phone number ID** and **WhatsApp Business Account ID**.
3. Leave the token empty to keep the current one, or paste a new token if the new number is in a different WhatsApp Business Account.
4. Click **Save number**.

The previous number is disconnected. The **AI replies** setting stays as it was.

## How your AI agent answers on WhatsApp

- Text messages and button replies are answered. Images, voice notes, and other media are not answered yet.
- Answers are sent as plain text. Source links appear as "Title: URL" after the answer.
- The AI replies in the language the customer writes in.
- After 24 hours without messages, the next message starts a new conversation. This matches WhatsApp's 24-hour customer service window.

### Current limitations

- Your team cannot reply to WhatsApp conversations from the Conversations inbox yet. When a customer asks for a person on WhatsApp, the AI keeps answering. Share another way to reach your team (such as an email address or phone number) in your handoff procedure.
- IrisAgent does not send WhatsApp template messages, so it never messages a customer first or outside the 24-hour window.
- Each WhatsApp number can be connected to only one IrisAgent account.

## Troubleshooting

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Enter the numeric Phone number ID…" | Copy the **Phone number ID** from WhatsApp Manager, not the phone number. |
| "invalid accessToken" | The token is wrong, expired, or revoked. Generate a new system user token and try again. |
| "the access token cannot access this WhatsApp phone number" | The token's system user isn't assigned to this WhatsApp Business Account, or lacks the `whatsapp_business_messaging` and `whatsapp_business_management` permissions. Assign the account to the system user in Business Settings and generate a new token. |
| "this WhatsApp phone number is connected to another customer" | The number is already connected to a different IrisAgent account. Disconnect it there first, or [contact us](mailto:contact@irisagent.com). |
| The card is read-only | Only dashboard admins can connect a WhatsApp number. Ask an admin. |

### Customers aren't getting replies

- Check that the card shows **Connected**, not **Paused**.
- Check the number's **Quality rating** and messaging limits in WhatsApp Manager. Meta may restrict a number with a low rating.
- Make sure the number isn't also connected to another WhatsApp tool, such as the WhatsApp Business app or another provider.
