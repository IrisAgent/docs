IrisAgent Helpdesk lets your team work customer conversations directly in the IrisAgent dashboard, without a separate ticketing tool. Your AI agent answers first on every channel. When a customer needs a person, the conversation lands in the **Conversations** inbox, where a teammate can join the chat, email the customer, leave internal notes, and mark it resolved.

<Note>
Already use Zendesk, Salesforce, Intercom, Freshchat, or another helpdesk? You do not need IrisAgent Helpdesk. IrisAgent can hand conversations to your existing tool instead. See [Connect your helpdesk](/guides/Connecting-Your-Helpdesk).
</Note>

## Channels at a glance

| Channel | AI agent answers | Your team can reply from Conversations | Set up in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Website chat widget | Yes | Yes, through [live chat](/helpdesk/Live-Chat) | **Deploy → Chat channels** |
| Email | Not applicable | Yes, outbound [email replies](/helpdesk/Email) to a conversation's contact | **Deploy → Email channels** |
| WhatsApp | Yes | Not yet. Conversations are AI-answered | **Deploy → Text channels** |
| Voice | Yes | Leave internal notes or send an email | **Deploy → Voice channels** |
| SMS | Outbound campaigns only | No | **Deploy → Text channels** (see [Outbound SMS](/deploying-irisagent/Outbound-SMS)) |

## Set up your helpdesk

Follow these steps in order. Steps 2 to 5 need an IrisAgent **admin**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your knowledge">
    Your AI agent answers from your knowledge base. Add at least one source under **Data Sources**, such as [Public Websites](/data-sources/Public-Websites) or [Upload Content](/data-sources/Upload-Content), and test answers in the Playground.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy the website chat widget">
    Add the IrisAgent chat widget to your website. See [Chatbot on Website](/deploying-irisagent/Chatbot-on-Website).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on live chat">
    In the chatbot's **Handoff** tab, choose **Start a live chat** and set the destination to the **IrisAgent inbox**. See [Live chat](/helpdesk/Live-Chat#turn-on-live-chat).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up email replies (optional)">
    Verify a sender address on your company's domain under **Deploy → Email channels**. See [Email replies](/helpdesk/Email).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect WhatsApp (optional)">
    Connect your WhatsApp Business number under **Deploy → Text channels**. See [WhatsApp](/helpdesk/WhatsApp).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Invite the people who will answer customers. See [Invite your team](#invite-your-team) below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Invite your team

1. Open the user menu and choose **Settings**.
2. In the **Dashboard** section, open the **Users** card and click **Manage**.
3. Enter one or more email addresses, separated by commas.
4. Choose a role and click **Invite Users**.

You can change a teammate's role or remove them from the same panel.

### Roles

IrisAgent has two dashboard roles:

| Task | Non-admin | Admin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| View and search Conversations | Yes | Yes |
| Take over, reply to, and end live chats | Yes | Yes |
| Add internal notes, set status, owner, and priority, mark resolved | Yes | Yes |
| Send email replies from a conversation | Yes | Yes |
| Configure chatbot handoff and live chat | No | Yes |
| Configure the email sender, SMS, and WhatsApp | No | Yes |
| Edit or merge contacts | No | Yes |
| Invite admins, change roles, remove users | No | Yes |

New invites default to **non-admin**. Only an admin can invite another admin.
