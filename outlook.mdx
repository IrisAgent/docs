## Sign in to IrisAgent

Go to the [IrisAgent Dashboard](https://web.irisagent.com/) and sign in with Google, Microsoft, or Okta SSO.

## Choose Outlook as your ticket source

You can connect Outlook during onboarding or later from the dashboard.

- **During onboarding:** on the **Connect your first data source** step, choose **Ticketing system**, then pick **Outlook** from the list of providers.
- **Later, or to add Outlook alongside an existing source:** go to **Data Sources → Ticketing System** in the left navigation and pick **Outlook**.

<br />

## Connect the mailbox

On the **Connect with Outlook** form, provide the following details:

- **Mailbox Email**: The email address of the mailbox used for ticket ingestion. Use a shared or support inbox, for example `support@yourcompany.com`.
- **Tenant ID**: Your Azure account identifier. You can find your Tenant ID by following [Microsoft's guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sharepoint/find-your-office-365-tenant-id).

Click **Connect**. You will be redirected to Microsoft OAuth to authorize IrisAgent. The following permissions are requested:

- `Mail.ReadWrite` lets IrisAgent read incoming mail and save AI-generated replies in the Outlook **Drafts** folder.
- `Mail.Send` lets IrisAgent send a reply automatically when you choose that delivery method.
- `offline_access` keeps the connection active without requiring you to sign in for every mailbox sync.

<Note>
We recommend using a dedicated service account for the OAuth connection rather than a personal Microsoft account. This ensures uninterrupted access if the individual account owner leaves the organization.
</Note>

After you authorize, IrisAgent returns you to the dashboard and starts the initial backfill of your mailbox. Depending on volume this can take a few minutes to a few hours.

<br />

## Reconnect an existing Outlook mailbox to enable drafts

Outlook connections created before draft replies were available might not include the `Mail.ReadWrite` permission. If **Save as Outlook draft** is unavailable in **Automate → Case Triggers**, reconnect the existing mailbox once:

1. Open [Change Integration User](https://web.irisagent.com/manage-integration-user).
2. Confirm the connected platform is **Outlook**.
3. Enter the same mailbox email address and Tenant ID used by the existing connection.
4. Click **Connect**, sign in to the existing Microsoft account, and approve the requested permissions.

Reconnect from the same IrisAgent dashboard. It updates the existing Outlook connection and does not create a second dashboard or move your data.

<Note>
Use the same mailbox and Microsoft tenant unless you intentionally want IrisAgent to connect as a different integration user.
</Note>

For reply configuration, see [Outlook AI Email Replies](/deploying-irisagent/Outlook).

<br />

## Connect more data sources (optional)

To connect more platforms, such as Jira, Confluence, or Salesforce, go to **Data Sources** in the left navigation and open the relevant section (Knowledge Bases, Development, CRM, and so on).
