Sign up at [web.irisagent.com](https://web.irisagent.com). The onboarding wizard opens on your first login.

## Step 1: Connect your first data source

Pick one path:

- **Ticketing system** (Zendesk, Freshdesk, Salesforce, HubSpot, Jira, Zoho, or Outlook): connect and IrisAgent imports your tickets, macros, and knowledge base articles. Best for suggested resolutions, agent copilot, and AutoQA.
- **Website link**: paste your URL and IrisAgent builds an AI agent from your public site and help docs. Best for chat deflection.

Not ready? Select **Not ready yet, speak to a human** to book a 20-minute demo.

## Step 2: Deploy

The wizard's last step offers:

- **Embed the chat widget** on your website (the snippet is generated for you)
- **Install the agent copilot** sidebar in your ticketing system
- **Invite teammates** to the workspace

For all options, see [Website chat widget](/deploying-irisagent/Chatbot-on-Website).

## Step 3: Add more data sources

From the dashboard, add:

- Knowledge bases (Confluence, Notion, Zendesk Guide)
- File uploads (PDF, DOCX, plain text)
- Q&A pairs and custom APIs

See the Connect your data section, starting with [Zendesk](/data-sources/Zendesk).
