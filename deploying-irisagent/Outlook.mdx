IrisAgent can reply to incoming customer emails in your connected Outlook mailbox using your knowledge base and past tickets. Replies stay on the original thread and come from the connected mailbox. You choose to save them as drafts or send them automatically, for all emails or only those matching your conditions.

Outlook uses the same **Case Triggers** engine as tickets. See [Tickets](/deploying-irisagent/Tickets) for all conditions, actions, and variables.

## Prerequisites

1. Your mailbox is connected. See the [Outlook connection guide](/outlook).
2. The connection includes `Mail.ReadWrite` (drafts) and `Mail.Send` (automatic replies), plus their `.Shared` variants for a shared mailbox. New connections request all of these.
3. You are an IrisAgent admin.

<Note>
If **Save as Outlook draft** is unavailable, [reconnect the mailbox once](/outlook#reconnect-an-existing-outlook-mailbox-to-enable-drafts) to approve `Mail.ReadWrite`.
</Note>

## How emails become tickets

- A **new inbound email** becomes a ticket.
- A **reply in that conversation** becomes a comment on the ticket.

Triggers run on tickets, so IrisAgent replies to the email that starts a conversation, not to every reply.

## Delivery method

- **Save as Outlook draft:** saves the reply in the mailbox's **Drafts** folder. Nothing goes to the customer until an agent sends it.
- **Send automatically:** sends the reply as soon as the trigger runs, with no agent review.

New setups with draft permission default to **Save as Outlook draft**. Existing triggers keep sending automatically until an admin changes them. Changing the method affects future runs only. It does not send drafts already in Outlook.

<Tip>
Start with drafts, or with Option 2 on a small slice of email, so agents can check answer quality before you send automatically.
</Tip>

## Option 1: Reply to all eligible emails

1. Go to **Automate** → **Case Triggers**.
2. On the **AI Email Replies** card, pick a **Delivery method**.
3. Turn on the **AI Email Replies** switch.

This creates the trigger **System: Case Deflection using AI**, which runs on every ticket that has an AI answer. Emails without an answer are left for your agents. Use the same switch to turn it off.

## Option 2: Reply only to matching emails

1. Go to **Automate** → **Case Triggers** and click **Create New Trigger**.
2. Click **Add conditions** and add your conditions (see below).
3. Under **Actions**, add **AI email reply**.
4. Choose a **Delivery method**.
5. In the text box, add `{{irisgpt.case.answer}}`. It is replaced by the AI answer and its source links. You can add your own text around it.
6. Name the trigger with the pencil icon, then click **Add New Trigger**.

Emails that match and have an AI answer get a reply. Matching emails without an answer are skipped and retried if an answer is generated later.

| Condition | Outlook meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Custom Field | A custom field on the IrisAgent case |
| Subject | The email subject |
| Ticket Content | The email body, without formatting |

## What the reply looks like

- HTML: the AI answer, then a bulleted list of its source links, plus any text you added around `{{irisgpt.case.answer}}`.
- On the existing thread, with the subject and threading intact.
- From the connected mailbox, not an IrisAgent address.

## Guardrails

- **No answer, no reply.** IrisAgent never drafts or sends without an AI answer.
- **One reply per conversation, per action.** A trigger action does not run twice on the same conversation.
- **Recent email only.** Triggers run on recent activity, not your whole mailbox history.

## Other variables

| Variable | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `{{agent.name}}` | Name of the assigned agent |
| `{{ticket.sentiment}}` | Detected ticket sentiment |
