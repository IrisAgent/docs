The Copilot sidebar shows AI-suggested resolutions (with Refine and Send to Draft on Zendesk), case summaries, sentiment, similar cases, linked Jira issues, and more, right in the ticket view. For a feature tour, see [Agent Assist / Copilot App](/agent-copilot).
