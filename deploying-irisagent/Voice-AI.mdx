IrisAgent Voice AI answers customer calls using your knowledge base, past tickets, and procedures.

## Enable Voice AI

The IrisAgent team turns on Voice AI and provisions a phone number for you. Contact your IrisAgent point of contact or [email us](mailto:contact@irisagent.com).

## Try it

Open the [Voice AI page](https://web.irisagent.com/voice-ai) and either:

- Click **Start Conversation** to talk to the agent in your browser (fastest for testing), or
- Call the phone number shown on the page.

You can give this number to customers as is. No extra setup is needed.

## Configure the voice assistant

1. Go to **Deploy** > **Voice channels** ([link](https://web.irisagent.com/manage-chatbot/voice)) and click **Configure** on the **Voice AI** card.
2. On the **Inbound assistant** tab, update the settings below.
3. Click **Save voice settings**. Changes apply to new calls.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Voice** | The voice callers hear. |
| **Language** | **English**, **Multilingual (auto-detect)** (replies in the caller's language and follows switches), or **Arabic** (keeps every call in Arabic). |
| **Allow callers to interrupt the greeting** | Lets callers speak before the greeting ends. |
| **Initial greeting** | First message of the call. Required, up to 1,000 characters. |
| **Response timing** | How fast the assistant replies: **Fast**, **Balanced**, or **Patient**. |
| **Interruption sensitivity** | How easily callers can talk over it: **Easy to interrupt**, **Balanced**, or **Resistant**. |
| **End call after silence** | 10 to 600 seconds. |
| **Maximum call duration** | 1 to 720 minutes. |
| **Record calls** | Stores recordings for review. Make sure you comply with notice and consent laws. |

If the assistant uses a custom or non-IrisAgent voice, it shows as unsupported. Pick a supported voice before saving other settings.

If someone else changes the assistant while you edit, IrisAgent will not overwrite their change. Review the reloaded settings, reapply your edits, and save.

## Control what Voice AI does with procedures

Voice AI answers from your knowledge base and past tickets by default. For specific scenarios (collect an order ID and look it up, check refund eligibility, hand off to a team), create a procedure:

1. Go to **Automate** > **Procedures** ([link](https://web.irisagent.com/automation/categories)) and click **Create New Procedure / Q&A**.
2. Select **Voice** as the channel.
3. Enter a name, when to use it, and the actions to take.
4. Choose **Instructions for AI Agent** to collect information or run actions. Add fields to **Information to collect**.
5. Select **Inbound** under **Voice calls**, set **Status** to **Enabled**, and click **Save**.

Procedures override knowledge base answers when the caller's request matches. See [Workflows](/configuring-ai-and-automation/Workflows) for writing procedures and calling your own APIs.

## Create tickets from calls

If Zendesk is your ticketing provider, procedures include **Create Zendesk ticket** under **Actions > Connected actions**. No Custom API is needed.

1. Add fields such as **Caller name**, **Caller email**, **Caller phone**, and **Issue summary** to **Information to collect**.
2. Insert `{{Create Zendesk ticket}}` where the ticket should be created.

The collected email becomes the requester. All non-empty collected values go into the description. See [Create Zendesk tickets from a procedure](/configuring-ai-and-automation/Workflows#create-zendesk-tickets-from-a-procedure).

## Transfer calls to a human

Call transfer sends the caller to your team only when IrisAgent requests escalation (a procedure calls for it, or IrisAgent can't answer). Enabling it does not transfer every call.

### Enable call transfer

1. In [Voice AI settings](https://web.irisagent.com/manage-chatbot/voice), open the **Call transfer** tab.
2. Turn on **Human handoff**.
3. Enter the **Transfer phone number** in international format (for example `+14155550188`).
4. Optional: enter an **Extension** (up to 10 digits), dialed after the destination answers.
5. Enter the **Message before transfer** (up to 1,000 characters). Default: "I'll connect you with a human agent now."
6. Click **Save transfer settings**. New calls use the saved settings.

<Warning>
  Saving a number while **Human handoff** is off does not enable transfers. Use a direct agent, ring group, or contact-center number. Never use the Voice AI number or any number that routes back to it, or you will create a call loop.
</Warning>

These settings apply to the **inbound** assistant only.

### Request handoff in a procedure

In a procedure with **Instructions for AI Agent**, click **Actions > Agent actions > Escalate to human** where handoff should happen. This inserts `[ESCALATE]`. Write the condition next to it:

```text
1. Ask the caller what they need help with.
2. If they ask to speak with a person, [ESCALATE]
3. Otherwise, follow the troubleshooting steps for their issue.
4. If the issue is unresolved and the caller wants human help, [ESCALATE]
```

The procedure must include **Voice** and **Inbound** and be **Enabled**. Writing "I'll transfer you" without the action does not transfer the call.

### How transfer works

IrisAgent does an immediate (blind) transfer. It plays your message and connects the caller without checking that an agent is available.

- Unanswered calls follow your phone system's routing (for example voicemail). Set business hours and queues there.
- Extra telephony charges may apply.
- Transfers do not create a Zendesk ticket. To do both, put **Create Zendesk ticket** before the escalate action.

### Test it

1. Confirm **Human handoff** is on and the number is correct.
2. Place a real phone call to the Voice AI number. A browser test does not check the phone route.
3. Trigger your handoff condition (for example, ask for a person).
4. Confirm you hear the message and the destination rings (and the extension works, if set).
5. Review the call in **Activity** > **Conversations**.

No transfer? Check the toggle, the procedure's status and inbound voice availability, and whether the condition was met. Transfer starts but no one answers? Check the number, extension, queue, and voicemail routing.

## Review calls

Every call appears in **Activity** > **Conversations**, with the recording, transcript, and procedures that ran. See [Conversations inbox](/helpdesk/Conversations-Inbox).
