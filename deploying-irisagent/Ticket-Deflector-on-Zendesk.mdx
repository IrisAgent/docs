The Ticket Deflector is a small script for your Zendesk Help Center. On the **Submit a request** page, it reads what the customer types and suggests AI answers and articles before they submit a ticket.

## Prerequisites

- Admin access to your Zendesk Help Center (Suite Growth or above, or Guide Professional or above)
- Access to the IrisAgent dashboard

## Add the script to your theme

1. In Zendesk, click the **Zendesk Products** icon in the top bar and select **Knowledge**.
2. In **Knowledge admin**, click **Customize design** in the sidebar.
3. Click **Customize** on your theme, then **Edit code**.
4. Open `new_request_page.hbs` under **Templates**. Paste this after the last closing `</div>` (outside `<div class="container">`):

```html
<script
  src="https://chatbot.irisagent.com/scripts/irisagent-deflector.js"
  data-api-key="YOUR_IRIS_AGENT_API_KEY"
  defer>
</script>
```

5. Replace `YOUR_IRIS_AGENT_API_KEY` with your **Business ID** from **Deploy → API Keys** in the [IrisAgent dashboard](https://web.irisagent.com/manage-chatbot/api-keys).
6. The deflector replaces the built-in search bar, so delete the whole `<div class="search-container">` element.

<Accordion title="Full example of new_request_page.hbs (with search-container removed)">
```html
<div class="container-divider"></div>
<div class="container">
  <nav class="sub-nav">
    {{breadcrumbs}}
  </nav>

  <h1>
    {{t 'submit_a_request'}}
    <span class="follow-up-hint">
      {{follow_up}}
    </span>
  </h1>

  <div id="main-content" class="form">
    {{request_form wysiwyg=true}}
  </div>
</div>

<script
  src="https://chatbot.irisagent.com/scripts/irisagent-deflector.js"
  data-api-key="YOUR_IRIS_AGENT_API_KEY"
  defer>
</script>
```
</Accordion>

## Test and publish

1. Click **Save**, then **Preview**, and open the **Submit a request** page.
2. Type a test subject and description (at least 15 characters in total). Suggested answers should appear before you submit.
3. Back on the Themes page, click **Set as live theme**.
