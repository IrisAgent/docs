## Prerequisites

- An Ozonetel CloudAgent account with admin access
- A DID (phone number) in Ozonetel that receives customer calls
- Access to the CloudAgent Admin Console to edit campaigns and IVR settings

## Step 1: Sign up for IrisAgent

Sign up by connecting your ticketing platform or a public website as a knowledge source. The IrisAgent team gives you an external phone number for your Voice AI.

## Step 2: Route calls in Ozonetel CloudAgent

### Create an IVR flow

1. Sign in to the [Ozonetel CloudAgent Admin Console](https://ozonetel.com/cloudagent/).
2. Go to **Admin > Configurations > IVR Flows** and click **New IVR Flow**.
3. Enter a **Flow Name** (for example, "IrisAgent Voice AI") and set **Flow Type** to **IVR**.
4. Enable **Transfer** and set the flow to transfer calls to the IrisAgent phone number. Include the country code (for example, +91 or +1).
5. Click **Save**.

### Create an inbound campaign

1. Go to **Campaigns**, click **Create Campaign**, and choose **Inbound Campaign**.
2. Enter a **Campaign Name**.
3. Select the **DID** your customers dial.
4. Under **IVR Flows**, select the flow you just created.
5. Set **Dispositions** and **Wrap-up Time** as needed, then click **Save**.
6. Click **Start/Stop** to activate the campaign. Its status changes from "READY" to "RUNNING".

### (Optional) Register the IrisAgent number

If Ozonetel needs the IrisAgent number registered for routing:

1. Go to **Admin > Configurations > Manage Numbers** and select **Phone Numbers**.
2. Click **New Number**, enter the IrisAgent number with country code, and click **Save**.

## Step 3: Test

1. Call your campaign's DID from an external phone.
2. Confirm you hear the IrisAgent Voice AI greeting.
3. Ask a few common customer questions and check the answers.
