AI Search adds a short AI answer above your website's search results, so customers get a direct answer instead of only a list of links.

## Set it up

1. Go to **AI Search**, click **Deployment settings**, then open the **Website Search Widget** card.
2. Choose a mode: **Basic usage**, **Public product mode** or **Specific product ID** (then pick the product).
3. Copy the generated snippet and paste it into the **body** of your website. The basic snippet looks like this:

   ```html
   <div id="iris-widget"></div>
   <script src="https://chatbot.irisagent.com/scripts/widget.iife.js"></script>
   <script>
     window.IrisSearchWidget.render({
       apiKey: '<API_KEY>',
       container: '#iris-widget',
     });
   </script>
   ```

The AI Search widget now appears on your website.
