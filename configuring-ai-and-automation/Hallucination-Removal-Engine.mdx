The Hallucination Removal Engine blocks answers whose claims are not supported by your indexed content. If a statement cannot be backed by a retrieved source, the answer is not sent as a resolved reply. It is always on. There is no separate toggle.

## How it works

For every answer (chat, ticket note, copilot suggestion or API response), IrisAgent:

1. **Retrieves** relevant articles, tickets and procedures.
2. **Generates** a draft from those sources.
3. **Checks** that each material claim matches the source text.
4. **Delivers** the answer with citations, or **declines or asks a clarifying question** if it fails.

See [Understanding grounded answers](/configuring-ai-and-automation/Understanding-Grounded-Answers) for more on this loop.

IrisAgent also tests every model and prompt change against two eval sets: questions the AI should resolve, and questions it should decline. Changes that raise resolution at the cost of fabrication do not ship.

**"Zero hallucinations"** means unsupported claims are not delivered as grounded answers. IrisAgent prefers a decline, a clarifying question or a handoff over a confident guess. When automation rate and grounding conflict, grounding wins.

## What you can configure

You make the engine more effective by improving its inputs and adding QA:

| Area | What to do | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Knowledge coverage | Connect your KB, Confluence, public docs and uploads | [Data sources](/data-sources/Zendesk), [Upload Content](/data-sources/Upload-Content) |
| Product scope | Segment content by product so the wrong product's articles are not used | [Product Segmentation](/configuring-ai-and-automation/Product-Segmentation) |
| Term alignment | Map customer wording to your KB vocabulary | [Search Term Rewrites](/configuring-ai-and-automation/Search-Term-Rewrites) |
| Procedures | Enable or write procedures for common flows | [Onboarding](/guides/onboarding), [Workflows](/configuring-ai-and-automation/Workflows) |
| Post-delivery QA | Add AutoQA rules that flag made-up features, pricing or policies | [AutoQA](/configuring-ai-and-automation/AutoQA) |
| Spot checks | Run Answer Grounding QA on a delivered answer | [Support Analyst](/support-analyst) |

A good first AutoQA rule (category **Resolution Quality**):

> AI agent must not fabricate or hallucinate product features, pricing, or policies

## Limits

- **Stale or wrong articles still pass.** An answer can be faithful to an outdated or off-topic article. Support Analyst labels this **wrong article** (a retrieval or KB problem).
- **Grounded answers can be incomplete.** An answer can cite the right article and still miss a step. Support Analyst labels this **incomplete**.
- **KB gaps show up as declines.** Fix them with new articles or [Knowledge Suggestions (AutoKB)](/configuring-ai-and-automation/AutoKB), not by loosening the check.
- **No always-on audit yet.** Auditing every AI answer automatically is on the roadmap. Use AutoQA and Support Analyst spot checks today.
- **Agents can edit suggestions.** Copilot suggestions are starting points. Agents are accountable for what they send.
