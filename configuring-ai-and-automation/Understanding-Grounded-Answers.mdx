A grounded answer is built only from knowledge connected to your account (articles, tickets, procedures, and other indexed sources) and cites those sources. If IrisAgent cannot find support for an answer, it declines or asks a clarifying question instead of guessing.

## How grounding works

For each question, IrisAgent:

1. Retrieves relevant articles, tickets, or procedures.
2. Generates the reply from that material only.
3. Shows the sources so a person can check the reply.

This loop (retrieve, generate from sources, refuse when support is missing) is how IrisAgent reaches validated answer accuracy above 95% on its accuracy evals.

## Sources IrisAgent checks

| Source type | Examples | Docs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ticketing system | Zendesk, Salesforce, Intercom, Freshworks, HubSpot, Jira Service Desk, Zoho Desk | [Connect your helpdesk](/guides/Connecting-Your-Helpdesk) |
| Built-in helpdesk KB | Help center articles synced from your ticketing system | [Ticketing System KB](/data-sources/Ticketing-Provider-KB) |
| Knowledge bases | Confluence, public websites, uploaded CSV/PDF | [Confluence](/data-sources/Confluence), [Public Websites](/data-sources/Public-Websites), [Upload Content](/data-sources/Upload-Content) |
| Procedures and workflows | Procedures and automations you enable | [Onboarding](/guides/onboarding), [Workflows](/configuring-ai-and-automation/Workflows) |

IrisAgent uses only content indexed for your account, never another customer's data. If you segment by product, retrieval is limited to that product's knowledge. See [Product Segmentation](/configuring-ai-and-automation/Product-Segmentation).

## When IrisAgent refuses

1. **No matching source.** Nothing indexed supports a confident answer, so IrisAgent does not invent policy, pricing, or product behavior.
2. **Out of scope.** The question is about something your sources do not cover, such as a product line you have not indexed.
3. **Missing context.** The ticket or chat lacks details needed to find the right article. IrisAgent asks a clarifying question.
4. **Failed faithfulness check.** Pre-delivery validation finds a claim the cited sources do not support, so the answer is not delivered as grounded. See [Hallucination Removal Engine](/configuring-ai-and-automation/Hallucination-Removal-Engine).

A clean handoff to a human is better than a confident wrong answer.

## Check grounding after delivery

In [Support Analyst](/support-analyst), ask it to check the grounding of the AI answer for a case or chat ID. It grades:

- **Retrieval relevance:** were the cited articles about the question?
- **Grounding faithfulness:** is every claim supported by those articles?

Failures are labeled as hallucination, wrong article, incomplete answer, or knowledge-base gap, so you know whether to fix the model, retrieval, or the KB.

## Check grounding before a change goes live

[Evals](/configuring-ai-and-automation/Evals) replay saved customer conversations against your agent and check that every claim in every reply is backed by a cited source. Unsupported claims are highlighted in the transcript. Run them after content or procedure changes, or on a procedure before you save it.
