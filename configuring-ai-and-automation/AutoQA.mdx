AutoQA scores every support conversation (tickets, chats, and Voice AI calls) against rules you write in plain English. Each conversation gets a QA score from 0 to 5 and a pass or fail for every rule, with explanations.

To test your AI agent on saved scenarios before a change goes live, see [Evals](/configuring-ai-and-automation/Evals). To test the LLM calls your own apps make, see [IrisEvals](/autoeval).

## Create a rule

1. Open **[QA Rules](https://web.irisagent.com/auto-qa/rules)** and click **Create New Rule**.
2. Fill in the form:

   | Field | What to enter |
   |---|---|
   | **Short Name** | Optional label for tables and filters, such as "Identity verification". |
   | **Rule Description** | What to check, in plain English. Example: *"Flag conversations where the agent did not verify the customer's identity before making account changes."* |
   | **Category** | Compliance, Tone & Empathy, Resolution Quality, Process Adherence, Escalation, or Custom. |
   | **Severity** | Critical, Warning, or Info. |
   | **Channels** | Which conversations the rule scores: Tickets, Chats, or Voice AI calls. For Voice AI calls, you can narrow to specific call types or campaigns. |
   | **Score weight (%)** | Optional. Weights are normalized per review, so they don't need to total 100. |

3. Click **Create Rule**. New rules are active and apply to new conversations.

### Example rules

| Category | Rule |
|---|---|
| Compliance | Agent must verify customer identity before making any account changes |
| Tone & Empathy | Agent must maintain a professional and empathetic tone throughout the conversation |
| Process Adherence | Agent should offer a follow-up or ask if the customer needs anything else before closing |
| Resolution Quality | AI agent must not fabricate or hallucinate product features, pricing, or policies |

## Manage rules

The QA Rules page shows **Active Rules**, **Rule Evaluations**, **Avg Rule Pass Rate**, and **Critical Rules Below 90%**. Filter the table by **All**, **Active**, or **Inactive**, or search.

From a rule's actions menu (three dots):

- **Edit:** change the rule and see its **Pass Rate (All time)**, **Cases Evaluated**, **Status**, and **Created by**. Click **Save Changes**.
- **Disable** or **Enable:** inactive rules stop scoring new conversations but are kept.
- **Delete:** permanently removes the rule and its evaluation history.

## Review results

Open **[QA Reviews](https://web.irisagent.com/auto-qa/reviews)**. The top cards show **Reviewed**, **Failures**, **Critical Failures**, and **Avg QA Score**.

Filter by channel, **Result** (Pass or Fail), **Rule**, **Severity**, **Agent**, or **Agent type**, or search.

Click a review to see:

- **QA Scorecard:** every rule checked, pass or fail, with an explanation for each failure.
- **Flagged Messages:** the messages that caused a violation. Chats and calls also show the full transcript.

### Score colors

| Metric | Green | Orange | Red |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pass Rate | 90% or above | 75% to 89% | Below 75% |
| QA Score | 4.0 or above | 3.0 to 3.9 | Below 3.0 |
