AutoKB drafts knowledge base articles from your resolved support cases. You review the drafts, then download them or publish them to Zendesk Guide or KnowledgeOwl.

## View drafts

Go to **Automate → Knowledge Suggestions** on the [IrisAgent dashboard](https://web.irisagent.com/autokb). The table shows each draft's **Name**, **Category**, **Source Case ID**, **Est. 7-day cases**, **Created At**, and **Status** (Draft, Reviewed, or Published). Once articles are published, **Published Provider** and **Published Article ID** columns also appear.

- Use the **All**, **Pending review**, and **Published** tabs to filter.
- Click a row to preview the full article.
- Open a row's **Actions** menu to **Download as Markdown**, **Download as PDF**, or **Mark as read** (which sets the status to Reviewed).

## Set up publishing

### Zendesk Guide

1. Go to **Data Sources** and, under **Knowledge Bases**, click the settings icon on the **Zendesk** card.
2. Turn on **Send AutoKB article drafts to Zendesk Guide**.
3. Fill in all four required fields:
   - **Section:** where articles are published. Changing it resets the locale.
   - **Locale:** the article language, such as `en-us`.
   - **User Segment:** who can view the articles.
   - **Permission Group:** who can manage the articles.
4. Click **Save**.

### KnowledgeOwl

1. Go to **Data Sources** and, under **Knowledge Bases**, click the settings icon on the **KnowledgeOwl** card.
2. Under **Configure KnowledgeOwl project for automatic article generation**, enter or select the **Project ID**.
3. Click **Save**.

The **Ingestion** settings in the same panel are separate. They control which KnowledgeOwl projects IrisAgent reads from.

## Publish an article

1. In **Knowledge Suggestions**, open the row's **Actions** menu and click **Publish as a draft article**.
2. Select the knowledge base.

The row then shows the **Published Provider** and a link to the article. The publish option appears only for unpublished articles and only when at least one knowledge base is configured.
