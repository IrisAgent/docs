title: "Segment Tickets and Chats by Product"
sidebarTitle: "Segment by product"

If you support more than one product, product segmentation makes IrisAgent answer each ticket or chat using only that product's knowledge. If you have a single product, skip this page.

A ticket (or chatbot) and an article are connected when they are assigned the **same product**. You set this up in **Settings** > **Products**:

| Card | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Product Catalog** | Creates the products you assign everywhere else |
| **Article Product Classifier** | Assigns articles to products by URL pattern |
| **Case Product Classifier** | Assigns incoming tickets to products |

All of this is optional. With no configuration, every ticket can use your full knowledge base.

## 1. Create your products

1. Go to **Settings** and click **Manage products** on the **Product Catalog** card.
2. Under **Create product**, enter a **Product name** and **Product ID** (a lowercase slug, for example `billing`). The ID is locked after creation.
3. Click **Create product**. Repeat for each product.

## 2. Assign knowledge to products

- **Whole knowledge base**: in **Data Sources**, set the **Default product** for the knowledge source. It is used when no URL rule matches.
- **Mixed help center**: use the **Article Product Classifier** to override the default for specific article URLs:
  1. In **Settings**, click **Configure** on the **Article Product Classifier** card.
  2. Click **Add Group**, then pick one or more **Products**.
  3. Add regex patterns matched against article URLs, for example `^https://help\.example\.com/billing/.*`.
  4. Repeat for each product, then click **Save**.

## 3. Assign tickets to products

1. In **Settings**, click **Configure** on the **Case Product Classifier** card.
2. Click **Add Group** and pick the **Product**.
3. Add any of these signals:
   - **Brand ID** (Zendesk only): exact match on the ticket's numeric Zendesk brand ID.
   - **Tags**: exact tags applied when the ticket was created.
   - **Keywords**: whole-word, case-insensitive match in subject or description (`bill` matches `Bill` but not `billing`).
   - **Regex patterns**: matched against subject and description.
4. Repeat for each product, then click **Save**.

Each Zendesk Brand ID must contain only digits and can belong to only one group. Leading and trailing spaces are trimmed on save.

<Warning>
  New mappings apply only to tickets ingested after you save. Existing tickets are not reclassified.
</Warning>

**Match order** when a ticket matches more than one product:

1. Zendesk Brand ID (Zendesk only)
2. Regex
3. Keyword
4. Tag

Within regex, keyword, and tag, the first matching group (in the order you arranged them) wins. Once a group is configured, the Case Product Classifier fully controls how that account's tickets get products.

## 4. Scope a chatbot to a product

1. Go to **AI Products** > **Chatbot**, click **Configure and deploy**, and open the chatbot configuration.
2. On the **Advanced** tab, under **Product segmentation**, pick the **Product**.
3. Click **Update Configuration**.

The chatbot now uses only articles assigned to that product.

<Warning>
  Only set a chatbot product if articles are assigned to that same product. If none are, every chat returns no answer. Choose **All products** to use your full knowledge base.
</Warning>

## Example

Products `billing` and `shipping`:

- **Knowledge**: URL rule `^https://help\.example\.com/billing/.*` for `billing`, and `^https://help\.example\.com/shipping/.*` for `shipping`.
- **Tickets**: `billing` has Brand ID `123456789`, keyword `invoice`, and regex `(?i)\b(invoice|payment)\b`. `shipping` has tag `shipping` and keyword `ship`.

A Zendesk ticket from brand `123456789` matches `billing` before IrisAgent checks its text or tags. A ticket from another brand saying "my payment failed" matches the `billing` regex. Both are answered from billing articles only.
