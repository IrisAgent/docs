IrisAgent groups incoming tickets and chats into topics and watches each topic for an unusual jump in volume. When a topic spikes, it shows up as a trending incident on the dashboard, and IrisAgent can alert your team by email and Slack.

Topic clustering is on by default for cases and chat. IrisAgent turns on alerting for your account.

## How detection works

1. **Clustering.** An LLM groups recent tickets and chats into specific topics (for example, "Invoice PDF fails to download"), each with a title, description, and matching conversations. New conversations reuse existing topics where they fit. Generic, marketing, spam, and automated notification streams are filtered out.
2. **Baseline.** For each topic, IrisAgent computes a rolling 30-day median and maximum of conversations per day. Days with no conversations count as zero. The baseline refreshes daily.
3. **Scoring.** A daily job scores each full calendar day. If real-time detection is on, a job also runs every minute on the current UTC day so far.
4. **Confirmation.** A topic becomes a spike only if it meets one of these rules:

   | Topic | Rule |
   |---|---|
   | Older than 4 days | At least the **absolute floor** (default 8 conversations) **and** at least the **threshold** times the baseline median (default 5x). |
   | Older than 4 days, busy | Sets a new 30-day high **and** is at least 3x the baseline median. |
   | 4 days old or newer | At least the **new cluster floor** (default 12 conversations). |

   Topics on your exclusion list are dropped. If more topics spike in one run than the cap (default 15), IrisAgent keeps the ones with the largest percentage increase.

5. **Lifecycle.** A confirmed spike becomes an **active** trending incident with the topic title, description, conversation count, first spiked time, and sample conversations. When a later run no longer sees the spike, it is marked **resolved**.

## Where you see them

- **Analytics → [Topics](https://web.irisagent.com/overview)** marks spiking topics as trending, with the volume trend and a link to the topic's detailed view.
- The **topic detailed view** lists affected conversations with links to your ticketing system.
- The **weekly briefing and digest email** call out new and trending topics.

## Alerts

When alerting is on, IrisAgent sends a notification listing new and trending topics, each with the conversation count, first seen time, and up to five sample conversation links.

| Channel | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Email** | Sent to users set up to receive category notifications. Cadence: real time, daily, weekly, or monthly. |
| **Slack** | Posted to your connected Slack channel with a button to the topic. Best effort; it never blocks the email. |

Each trending incident alerts at most once every 24 hours. Excluded topics never alert.

## Settings

IrisAgent manages these settings. Email [contact@irisagent.com](mailto:contact@irisagent.com) to change them.

| Setting | Default | What it controls |
|---|---|---|
| Topic clustering (cases) | On | Cluster support cases into topics. |
| Topic clustering (chat) | On | Cluster website and in-app chats into topics. |
| Real time detection | Off unless enabled | Run spike detection every minute on the current day. |
| Batch notifications | Off unless enabled | Daily, weekly, or monthly new and trending topic emails and Slack posts. |
| Real time notifications | Off unless enabled | Alert as soon as a real-time spike is confirmed. |
| Daily threshold | 5x | Multiple over baseline median for the daily job. |
| Real time threshold | 5x | Multiple over baseline median for the real-time job. |
| Absolute volume floor | 8 | Minimum spike-day conversations for a mature topic. |
| New cluster floor | 12 | Minimum spike-day conversations for a topic too new to have a baseline. |
| Max spikes per run | 15 | Cap on trending incidents per run, ranked by percentage increase. |
| Excluded categories | Empty | Topic titles that must never trend or alert. |

## Act on a trending incident

- Open the topic's detailed view to see every affected conversation.
- Ask [Support Analyst](/support-analyst) for a timeline, sentiment, and affected accounts.
- Use [AutoKB](/configuring-ai-and-automation/AutoKB) and [procedures](/configuring-ai-and-automation/Ticket-Tagging) to give agents and the AI a correct answer while the incident is live.
- Watch the volume trend on the Topics page to confirm it has resolved.

## Limits

- Only conversations that were clustered into a topic count toward a spike.
- Real-time scoring resets at midnight UTC. A burst that crosses midnight is confirmed on the second day, with the daily job as backup.
- Baselines and rules are per topic and per customer. Other customers never affect your detection.
