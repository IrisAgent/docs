---
title: "Search Term Rewrites"
description: "Rewrite the terms customers use so they match your knowledge base before the AI searches it"
---

Search Term Rewrites (called **Query Term Rewrites** in the dashboard) swap terms in a customer's question or case text for the wording your knowledge base uses, before the AI searches. Use them for abbreviations, slang, or shorthand. For example, rewrite "acct" to "account".

<Note>
Rewrites apply to **both chat and case answers**. They only change which articles the AI finds, not the answer text.
</Note>

## Add a rewrite rule

1. In the [IrisAgent dashboard](https://web.irisagent.com), go to **Deploy → Cases** and find **Query Term Rewrites**.
2. Check **Enable query term rewrites**.
3. Click **+ Add rule** and fill in:
   - **From:** the term customers type, such as `acct`.
   - **To:** the term your knowledge base uses, such as `account`.
   - **Whole word:** match only the complete word (`acct` won't match inside `accts`).
   - **Ignore case:** match any capitalization (`Acct`, `ACCT`, `acct`).
4. Add more rules with **+ Add rule**, or remove one with **×**.
5. Click **Save Rewrites**.

**Whole word** and **Ignore case** are on by default. Every rule needs a **From** term, and **From** and **To** must differ, or the rules won't save.

## Example rules

| From | To | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| acct | account | Common abbreviation |
| pw | password | Shorthand |
| icloud | invoicecloud | Maps a misheard or shortened term to your product name |
| cancel sub | cancel subscription | Expands shorthand to match article wording |

To change the tone and wording of answers instead, see [AI Response Style](/configuring-ai-and-automation/AI-Response-Style).
